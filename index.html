IELTS Practice

Prepare for all four sections.

Reading Practice - Question 1

"The Amazon rainforest, often referred to as the 'lungs of the Earth,' produces approximately 20% of the world's oxygen. However, recent studies suggest that due to deforestation, this percentage is gradually declining."

What is the main concern of scientists?

A) It produces too much oxygen
B) Deforestation is reducing its oxygen production
C) It is expanding too quickly
D) Scientists cannot study it properly

Question 2

What does "tipping point" mean?

A) A tourist spot
B) The highest point of a mountain
C) A critical threshold leading to irreversible change
D) A scientific measuring tool

Task 2 Writing Topic

Some people believe the best way to reduce crime is longer prison sentences. Others believe there are better alternatives. Discuss both views and give your opinion.

Listening Tips

  • 🎧 Practice listening to English podcasts daily
  • 📝 Take notes while listening
  • 🔑 Focus on keywords and synonyms
  • ⏰ Manage your time — you only hear it once
  • ✍️ Check spelling — wrong spelling means wrong answer

Part 1 - Common Questions

  • 1. Tell me about your hometown.
  • 2. What do you do for work or study?
  • 3. Do you enjoy reading?
  • 4. What kind of music do you like?
  • 5. How do you spend your weekends?

Part 2 - Cue Card

Describe a memorable trip you have taken.

  • Where you went
  • Who you went with
  • What you did there
  • Why it was memorable

1 minute to prepare, 2 minutes to speak.
