📖 Present Indefinite Tense — বাংলা অর্থসহ ব্যাখ্যা Present Indefinite Tense = বর্তমান কালের সাধারণ কাজ বোঝাতে ব্যবহৃত হয়। Structure: Positive Sentences Subject + verb (base form) + object/extensions Subject (He/She/It) + verb + s/es + object/extensions Ex- I eat rice. She reads a book. They play football. Negative Sentences Subject + do not + verb + object/extensions Subject (He/She/It) + does not + verb + object/extensions Ex- I do not eat meat. She does not like tea. They do not play cricket. Interrogative Sentences Do + subject + verb + object/extensions + ? Does + subject (He/She/It) + verb + object/extensions + ? Ex- Do you like coffee? Does she go to school? Do they live here?
