Like vs Like To – Grammar Test
📖 Like vs Like To — বাংলা অর্থসহ ব্যাখ্যা Like = পছন্দ করা Like to = করা পছন্দ করা Use of like: Positive Sentences I like = আমি পছন্দ করি You like = তুমি পছন্দ কর We like = আমরা পছন্দ করি They like = তারা পছন্দ করে Ex- I like mangoes You like this song We like the new teacher They like spicy food Negative Sentence I don't like = আমি পছন্দ করি না You don't like = তুমি পছন্দ কর না We don't like = আমরা পছন্দ করি না They don't like = তারা পছন্দ করে না Ex- I don't like cold weather You don't like this movie We don't like waiting They don't like vegetables Question Sentences Do I like = আমি কি পছন্দ করি? Do you like = তুমি কি পছন্দ কর? Do we like = আমরা কি পছন্দ করি? Do they like = তারা কি পছন্দ করে? Ex- Do I like coffee? Do you like my dress? Do we like the plan? Do they like the new house? Third person singular number: He likes = সে পছন্দ করে She likes = সে পছন্দ করে Rahim likes = রহিম পছন্দ করে It likes = এটি পছন্দ করে Ex- He likes cricket She likes flowers Rahim likes reading books The cat likes milk Use of like to: Positive Sentences I like to = আমি করতে পছন্দ করি You like to = তুমি করতে পছন্দ কর We like to = আমরা করতে পছন্দ করি They like to = তারা করতে পছন্দ করে He/She likes to = সে করতে পছন্দ করে Everyone likes to = সবার করতে পছন্দ Ex- I like to swim in the morning You like to play football He likes to watch movies at night She likes to sing in the shower We like to travel together They like to eat out on weekends Negative Sentence I don't like to = আমি করতে পছন্দ করি না He doesn't like to = সে করতে পছন্দ করে না She doesn't like to = সে করতে পছন্দ করে না Ex- I don't like to wake up early He doesn't like to do homework She doesn't like to eat fish Question Sentences Do I like to = আমি কি করতে পছন্দ করি? Does he like to = সে কি করতে পছন্দ করে? Does she like to = সে কি করতে পছন্দ করে? Ex- Do you like to read novels? Does he like to play chess? Does she like to cook?
